Case types

What we help with.

If your situation is not listed, report it anyway. The first step is always a free assessment of what happened and what may be possible.

Crypto and digital asset fraud

Fake trading platforms, wallet drainers, pig-butchering schemes, fraudulent token projects and exchange account takeovers.

  • Map wallet addresses and transaction flows
  • Preserve exchange, wallet and platform records
  • Engage exchanges, forensic partners and authorities
Bring: wallet addresses, transaction IDs, exchange emails, screenshots of the platform.

Bank transfer and payment fraud

Push-payment scams, invoice fraud, impersonation, payment-app scams and transfers made under pressure or deception.

  • Build a precise payment and contact timeline
  • Pursue recall, dispute or chargeback routes
  • Coordinate with banks and payment providers
Bring: bank statements, payment confirmations, the messages that led to the transfer.

Investment and Ponzi schemes

Fake fund managers, unlicensed advisers, guaranteed-return schemes, boiler-room fraud and collapsed investment structures.

  • Organise the investment and payment record
  • Identify entities, directors and linked accounts
  • Support regulatory and legal action where appropriate
Bring: contracts, statements, payment records, adviser names and company documents.

Romance and relationship scams

Long-term emotional manipulation that turns into requests for money, crypto, gift cards or "emergency" payments.

  • Preserve messages and payment evidence sensitively
  • Trace payments to accounts, apps or wallets
  • Coordinate reporting and recovery with discretion
Bring: chat logs, profile details, transfer records and any images or documents shared.

Online shopping and marketplace fraud

Goods that never arrive, counterfeit products, fake sellers and marketplace transactions that go wrong.

  • Build a case for platform protection and card disputes
  • Document seller identity and listing evidence
  • Pursue chargeback and seller accountability routes
Bring: order confirmations, listing screenshots, payment receipts and seller messages.

Business email compromise

Hacked inboxes, fake supplier invoices, payment diversion and CEO fraud targeting businesses and their customers.

  • Contain the breach and preserve email evidence
  • Trace diverted funds and coordinate with banks
  • Work with insurers, legal advisers and IT security
Bring: email headers, invoices, payment records, IT logs and any insurer correspondence.

Account takeover and unauthorised access

Hacked bank, email, social media or crypto accounts, SIM-swap attacks and unauthorised transactions.

  • Secure accounts and document unauthorised activity
  • Pursue provider disputes and fraud claims
  • Coordinate with platforms and law enforcement
Bring: login alerts, transaction records, provider correspondence and any device or SIM details.

Travel, ticket and booking scams

Fake holiday rentals, flight and concert tickets, visa services and travel agents that disappear after payment.

  • Document the booking, seller and payment trail
  • Pursue card, platform and travel-protection routes
  • Coordinate with booking platforms and authorities
Bring: booking confirmations, listings, payment receipts and all messages with the seller.
