Before you report

The basics.

I feel embarrassed that I fell for a scam. Is that normal?

Yes — and it is one of the most common things victims tell us. Fraud is designed to look convincing, and scammers are skilled at building trust. Falling for a scam is not a sign that you are foolish. It is a sign that someone deliberately deceived you.

You will not be judged here. Our job is to help you deal with what happened.

How soon should I report?

As soon as you can — but do not delay reporting because you do not have every document. If money was taken in the last 24 hours, contact your bank or payment provider first, then report to us. Speed can matter for payment recalls and disputes.

What if I do not have all the evidence?

Start with what you have. Screenshots, a bank statement, a receipt or a few messages are enough for an initial assessment. We can help you work out what else is needed and how to preserve it.

Can I report anonymously?

We need a safe way to contact you so we can acknowledge your report and ask follow-up questions. You can use a separate email address, ask us to contact you by email only, and tell us if there are safe times or channels to use. We will not publish your details.

Do you help businesses as well as individuals?

Yes. We handle business email compromise, invoice fraud, payment diversion and other commercial fraud. We can coordinate with your finance team, insurers, IT provider and legal advisers.

Do you take cases outside your home country?

We assess cases across jurisdictions and work with local legal and investigative partners where needed. Cross-border cases can be more complex and slower, so the first step is a free assessment of what is realistic in your situation.

Fees & engagement

What it costs.

How much does it cost to report a scam?

Nothing. Your first case assessment is free, whether or not we take the case. If we believe there is a viable route, we explain our proposed scope and fee in writing before any chargeable work begins.

Do you take a percentage of what is recovered?

Where the law permits, we can agree a contingency fee that is payable only from a successful recovery. If a contingency arrangement is not permitted in your jurisdiction, we will tell you and discuss lawful alternatives. Any arrangement is set out in writing and approved by you first.

Do you ever ask for an upfront payment?

No. We do not ask for upfront "recovery" payments, and we never ask for payment by gift card, cryptocurrency or wire transfer. We also never ask for remote access to your computer or phone.

What if you cannot help?

We will tell you clearly, explain why, and — where possible — point you to other routes such as your bank's complaints process, a regulator, the police or a local advice service. There is no fee for the initial assessment.

Are court fees or specialist reports included?

Third-party costs such as court fees, translation, forensic reports or local lawyer fees are separate. We disclose them and get your approval before they are incurred.

The process

What working with us is like.

How long does recovery take?

It varies widely. We aim to acknowledge new reports within one business day and complete an initial assessment within one to three business days. Tracing and recovery action can take weeks or months, depending on the payment method, the institutions involved and the jurisdiction. We give you a realistic timeline for your case rather than a generic promise.

Who will contact me?

A dedicated case manager. They are your single point of contact and will coordinate any specialists, banks, platforms or legal partners involved in your case.

How often will I hear from you?

We update you at meaningful milestones and respond to questions within one business day. You will never be left wondering whether your case has been forgotten. If there is no update, we will tell you that too.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. You can stop the engagement at any time, subject to any costs already agreed and incurred. We will confirm the position in writing and hand over your file on request.

What information will you need from me?

As much of the evidence as you can safely provide: payment receipts, bank statements, messages, emails, website addresses, wallet addresses and any police or regulator reference numbers. You can start with what you have and add more later.

Privacy & safety

Your information.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. We treat your report as confidential and only share information with the institutions, partners or authorities needed to assess or pursue your case — or where the law requires it. Our Privacy Policy explains this in detail.

Will you contact me first asking for money?

No. We do not make unsolicited calls or messages asking for payment. If someone contacts you claiming to be from Sentinel Recovery, verify them using the contact details on this website before sharing anything.

What should I do if someone claims to be from Sentinel Recovery?

Do not send money, passwords or identity documents. Ask for their name and case reference, then contact us directly using the official email or phone number on this site. We will confirm whether the person is genuine.

How is my data stored?

Access is limited to the people who need it for your case, and we keep information only for as long as necessary for the case and our legal obligations. You can ask us for a copy of your information or ask us to correct it. See the Privacy Policy for your rights.

Do you use WhatsApp, Telegram or social media to manage cases?

We may use email and phone as agreed with you. We do not ask for sensitive documents or payments through unofficial social media accounts. Always check that you are speaking to us through the contact details published on this site.

Recovery scams

How to spot a fake recovery service.

How do I know this is not a recovery scam?

Be cautious with any service that guarantees recovery, demands an upfront fee, asks for payment by gift card or crypto, wants remote access to your device, or pressures you to act immediately. We do none of those things. We put our scope and fees in writing and you can verify our company details before sharing anything.

Someone contacted me saying they can recover my money. What should I do?

Do not pay them or send identity documents. Check whether the company is real, search for independent reviews, and ask for a written contract and fee terms. If they pressure you or ask for crypto or gift cards, stop and report them to your local fraud authority.

Is it ever too late to recover money?

Sometimes, yes. The trail can go cold, funds can be withdrawn, and legal time limits may apply. That is why we are honest about timing. But "late" is not the same as "impossible" — the only way to know is to review the facts.

Should I pay a fee to release my recovered funds?

No. A demand to pay a "release fee", "clearance fee" or "tax" before recovered money is returned is a classic follow-up scam. Legitimate recovery does not work that way. Stop, do not pay, and contact us or your bank directly.

Legal & limits

What we are — and are not.

Are you a law firm?

No. We are a fraud recovery and case-management company. We do not provide legal advice. Where legal action is appropriate, we work with independent licensed lawyers in the relevant jurisdiction.

Can you guarantee I will get my money back?

No — and you should be cautious of anyone who says they can. Recovery depends on the facts, the payment method, the jurisdiction and how quickly you act. What we can guarantee is an honest assessment, a clear plan and a written fee agreement before any work begins.

Do you work with the police?

We encourage you to report the crime to the police and can coordinate with them where appropriate. We are not a law enforcement agency and cannot investigate or prosecute a crime ourselves.

Can you get my money back from a scammer directly?

No. We do not "hack" scammers, access their accounts or pressure them into refunds. Recovery works through legitimate channels: payment recalls, disputes, platform complaints, regulatory action and, where appropriate, legal proceedings.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. We will give you a straight answer, even if it is not the one you were hoping for.
