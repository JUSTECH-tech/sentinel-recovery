Money taken in the last 24 hours?

Speed matters. Do these five things before you do anything else.

First 24 hours

Do these five things now

  • Call your bank or payment provider. Ask them to recall the payment or freeze the account.
  • Stop all contact with the scammer. Do not send more money or pay a "release fee".
  • Preserve everything. Screenshots, emails, messages, receipts and wallet addresses.
  • Report to the police and your local fraud regulator. Keep the reference numbers.
  • Secure your accounts. Change passwords and turn on two-factor authentication.
Then let us help

Common questions

Straight answers, before you commit.

If your question is not here, our case team will answer it directly — no sales pitch.

Can you guarantee I will get my money back?

No — and you should be cautious of anyone who says they can. Recovery depends on the facts, the payment method, the jurisdiction and how quickly you act. What we can guarantee is an honest assessment, a clear plan and a written fee agreement before any work begins.

How much does it cost?

Your first case assessment is free. If we believe there is a viable route, we will explain our fee in writing and you decide whether to proceed. We do not ask for upfront "recovery" payments, gift cards or cryptocurrency.

How long does recovery take?

It varies widely. An initial assessment is usually completed within one business day. Tracing and bank or exchange enquiries can take weeks, and formal recovery action can take longer. We give you a realistic timeline for your case rather than a generic promise.

What information will you need from me?

As much of the evidence as you can safely provide: payment receipts, bank statements, messages, emails, website addresses, wallet addresses and any police or regulator reference numbers. You can start with what you have and add more later.

Do you work with cases outside your home country?

We assess cases across jurisdictions and work with local legal and investigative partners where needed. Cross-border cases can be more complex, so the first step is always a free assessment of what is realistic.

How do I know this is not a recovery scam?

We will never contact you first asking for money, never ask for your online banking password or one-time codes, and never pressure you to pay by gift card, crypto or wire transfer. We put our fees and scope in writing, and you can verify our company details before you share anything.
