Privacy policy

This policy explains what information we collect, why we need it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It applies to our website and our case-assessment service.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

Sentinel Recovery Ltd. (“Sentinel Recovery”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is an independent fraud recovery and case-management company. Our registered office is . You can contact us about privacy at .

We are not a law firm, bank, government agency or regulator. We do not provide legal advice.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers personal information we collect through this website, when you contact us, and when we assess or manage a case. It does not cover the practices of banks, payment providers, platforms, regulators, law enforcement or other third parties, even where we coordinate with them.

3. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, information created during your case, and limited technical information from your browser.

Information you give us

  • Your name, email address, phone number, country and preferred contact method.
  • Details about the scam, including dates, amounts, payment methods, descriptions, suspect details and platform names.
  • Evidence you upload or send, such as receipts, bank statements, messages, emails, screenshots, wallet addresses and transaction IDs.
  • Any special-support needs or contact-safety preferences you tell us about.
  • Correspondence with us and records of calls or meetings.

Information created during a case

  • Case notes, timelines, assessments, correspondence with institutions and partners, and records of actions taken.
  • Identity and verification information where we are legally required to check who you are.

Technical information

  • Standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps, where our hosting provider records them.
  • Information stored locally in your browser, such as a saved report draft. See section 10.

Please do not send us passwords, PINs, one-time codes or full payment-card numbers. We do not need them and you should never share them.

4. Why we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a free case assessment.
  • Assess whether a recovery route exists and, if you engage us, manage your case.
  • Communicate with you, keep records and provide updates.
  • Coordinate with banks, payment providers, platforms, regulators, law enforcement, forensic specialists and legal partners where needed.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, anti-money-laundering, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Improve our website and services, prevent fraud and misuse, and protect our legal rights.

Where data-protection law requires a lawful basis, we rely on one or more of the following: your consent; the performance of a contract with you or steps taken at your request before entering a contract; compliance with a legal obligation; and our legitimate interests in running and improving our business and preventing fraud, balanced against your rights.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only where it is necessary for your case, where you have asked us to, or where the law requires it. Recipients may include:

  • Banks, payment providers, card issuers and payment platforms.
  • Cryptocurrency exchanges, wallet providers and blockchain-analysis providers.
  • Regulators, law enforcement, courts and dispute-resolution bodies.
  • Independent lawyers, forensic specialists, translators and local partners engaged for your case.
  • Service providers who host our systems, manage email or provide professional services, under confidentiality and data-protection terms.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and auditors where necessary.
  • A buyer or successor in the event of a merger, acquisition or transfer of our business, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your details with advertisers.

6. International transfers

Fraud cases often cross borders. Your information may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from a country other than your own. Where we transfer personal information internationally, we use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law, such as contractual protections or transfers to countries recognised as providing adequate protection.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. For case records, this generally means for the duration of the case and a reasonable period afterwards to deal with follow-up queries, legal claims and regulatory obligations. Enquiry records that do not become cases are kept for a shorter period. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

8. How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. These include access controls, secure transmission where information is submitted to our case systems, staff confidentiality obligations and regular review of our processes.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and any relevant authority as required by law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete information, subject to legal and contractual retention obligations.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format.
  • Complain to your local data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, contact us at . We may need to verify your identity before acting. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

10. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It may store information locally in your browser to keep a saved report draft and to remember basic interface preferences. This information stays on your device until you clear it or submit the report. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings or the “Start fresh” link on the report page.

We load typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. Please see Google’s privacy policy for more information. If you prefer, you can block third-party font requests in your browser.

11. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it was last revised. If a change is material, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to your attention.

13. Contact and complaints

For questions about this policy or how we handle your information, contact or write to us at .

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the data-protection authority in your country or region. We will cooperate with any such complaint.

This policy is a general template and should be reviewed by a qualified privacy lawyer for your jurisdiction, business model and data-processing activities before publication.
