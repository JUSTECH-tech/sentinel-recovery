How it works

No mystery and no false promises. Here is exactly what happens when you report a scam to Sentinel Recovery — including the parts that depend on your case.

Free assessment One case manager Written terms
The process

From first report to final outcome.

We move at the pace your case allows. You will always know what stage you are at, what we are doing and what we need from you.

  1. 01

    You report what happened

    Complete a guided, confidential report. You can save a draft, upload evidence and choose how we contact you. If money was taken in the last 24 hours, contact your bank first — then come back.

    About 10 minutes
  2. 02

    Free case assessment

    A case manager reviews your report and evidence. We check the payment method, timing, jurisdiction and what routes are realistically available. If we cannot help, we will tell you clearly and point you to other options.

    Within 1 business day
  3. 03

    Investigation and tracing

    If there is a viable route, we preserve the evidence trail and work with banks, payment providers, exchanges, regulators and legal partners. You receive a written scope and fee agreement before any chargeable work begins.

    Typically days to weeks
  4. 04

    Recovery action

    We pursue the route that fits your case: payment recall, chargeback or dispute, platform complaint, regulatory report, civil recovery or legal referral. We coordinate the steps and keep you updated.

    Depends on the route
  5. 05

    Resolution and aftercare

    You receive a clear outcome in plain English: what was recovered, what was not, and what to do next. We also help you secure your accounts and reduce the risk of being targeted again.

    Clear close
What we need

Start with what you have.

You do not need a perfect file. Most people come to us with a mixture of screenshots, bank records and messages. We help you organise the rest.

  • A timeline. Rough dates are fine — when contact started, when payments were made and when you realised.
  • Payment evidence. Bank statements, receipts, transaction IDs, card statements or wallet addresses.
  • Communications. Emails, messages, call logs, profiles and website links. Keep the originals.
  • Suspect details. Names, usernames, phone numbers, email addresses, company names and wallet addresses.
  • Reference numbers. Any police, bank, regulator or platform case references you already have.
  • A safe way to contact you. Tell us if email only is safer, or if a trusted person helps you.

Honest limits

What we can — and cannot — do.

Knowing the limits protects you from false expectations. If something is outside our scope, we will say so.

What we can do

  • Assess whether a legitimate recovery route exists.
  • Preserve, organise and present the evidence clearly.
  • Engage banks, payment providers, exchanges and platforms.
  • Coordinate with regulators, police and legal partners where appropriate.
  • Keep you informed and document every step.

What we cannot do

  • Guarantee that money will be recovered.
  • “Hack” a scammer, access their accounts or promise magic.
  • Act as your lawyer or provide legal advice.
  • Force a bank or platform to reverse a payment.
  • Erase the scam from your credit record or history.
Fees

Fees, plainly.

You should never have to guess what something will cost. Here is how we structure our work.

Free assessment

Your first case assessment costs nothing, whether or not we take the case.

Written agreement

You receive a clear scope and fee agreement before any chargeable work begins.

Contingency where permitted

Where the law allows, we can agree a fee that is only payable from a successful recovery.

No pressure

We never ask for payment by gift card, cryptocurrency or wire transfer, and never demand remote access.

Where a contingency fee is not permitted in your jurisdiction, we will tell you and discuss lawful alternatives. Third-party costs, such as court fees or specialist reports, are disclosed and approved by you in advance.
